I'm driven by understanding how systems work, where they can break, how they can be secured, and how personal and sensitive information is protected in an increasingly connected world.

I'm especially interested in cybersecurity and IT roles where I can apply an investigative mindset to identify risks, analyze problems, and find practical solutions. I enjoy digging into the details, breaking complex problems down, and understanding the “why” behind how something works rather than simply looking for an answer.

Here's what I bring to the table:

  • Hands-on experience with cybersecurity, networking, and system administration concepts through labs and self-directed projects.
  • Practical experience with tools and technologies including Wireshark, Nmap, Burp Suite, Kali Linux, Cisco Packet Tracer, SQL, Python, and security monitoring tools.
  • Experience contributing to a real-world open-source web project through Git, branching, pull requests, and website content and UI improvements.

I also make a point of staying current with cybersecurity, technology, and privacy news so I can better understand how real-world threats and emerging technologies are shaping the field.

I'm naturally curious, detail-oriented, and persistent when solving problems. Whether I'm troubleshooting a technical issue, investigating how something works, or learning a new technology, I like to go beyond the surface and understand what's actually happening.

Outside of tech, I'm a bit of a nerd at heart. I enjoy motorsports, history, and the storytelling and world-building behind franchises like Star Wars, Transformers, and Harry Potter. I also enjoy photographing cars at shows and editing the photos afterward, which gives me a creative outlet while still letting me focus on detail and perspective. Working out is another part of my routine and helps me stay disciplined and consistent.

I'm currently seeking opportunities in IT and cybersecurity where I can continue developing my technical skills, contribute to real-world challenges, and grow into a career in the field. Let's connect!
