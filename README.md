# Logan Nguyen — IT & Cybersecurity Portfolio

Personal portfolio website showcasing my technical background, projects, hands-on learning, and professional experience as a Computer Information Systems graduate from Florida Gulf Coast University with a concentration in cybersecurity.

My long-term goal is to build a career in cybersecurity. As a recent graduate, I’m currently focused on getting my foot in the door in IT or a related technology role where I can gain professional experience, continue developing my technical skills, and build toward that goal.

🌐 **Live Site:** https://logan5607.github.io/logannguyenportfolio.github.io/

---

## Overview

This portfolio documents my development as an IT professional and cybersecurity-focused technologist. It highlights hands-on cybersecurity and networking labs, technical projects, education, professional experience, and the skills I’m continuing to develop.

My approach to technology is investigative and practical. I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying where problems or vulnerabilities exist, and figuring out how they can be addressed.

The site itself was built from scratch using HTML5 and CSS3 without frameworks, templates, or CSS libraries.

---

## Career Direction

**Long-Term Goal:** Cybersecurity

**Current Goal:** Gain professional experience in IT, technical support, networking, systems, or another technology-focused role that allows me to apply what I’ve learned, continue building practical skills, and grow toward a career in cybersecurity.

I’m particularly interested in opportunities where I can troubleshoot problems, work with technology, investigate issues, learn from experienced professionals, and contribute to real-world environments.
